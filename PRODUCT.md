# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary: technical decision-makers** — CTOs, VPs of Engineering, and senior architects at mid-market-to-enterprise companies evaluating agentic AI and Umbraco platform partners. They arrive skeptical: they've seen the AI hype cycle, they carry the risk if an autonomous system misbehaves, and they need proof of governance, resilience, and human oversight before they'll trust a vendor.

**Secondary: digital / marketing owners** — directors who own the website and want AI-driven capability but need a delivery partner they can defend to their own leadership.

Context of use: evaluating, comparing, building a shortlist. Often a first touch from a referral or search. The job to be done is *de-risking a decision* — leaving the page able to say "these people are serious, they think about what I'm afraid of, and I'd put them in front of my boss."

## Product Purpose

Nubis Digital builds and operates agentic AI and Umbraco systems under the banner **"Architectural Resilience for the Agentic Web."** The site exists to convert qualified technical buyers into a first conversation by demonstrating — not just asserting — that Nubis treats autonomy as something to be *governed*, not hyped. The live AI agent on the page is the proof: a working capability with visible human-oversight controls, not a stock illustration. Success = a credible technical lead books a project conversation.

## Brand Personality

Voice: **calm, trustworthy, human.** Three words: *grounded, candid, assured.* Nubis speaks like a senior engineer who has shipped the hard thing and isn't impressed by buzzwords — explains the mechanism, names the tradeoff, and never oversells. The emotional goal is to **convert AI anxiety into confidence**: the reader should feel the team is the adult in the room.

The visual identity is already committed and precise (paper/ink, cobalt "signal," violet "peri," Playfair + Inter + JetBrains Mono, governance/oversight motifs). The "calm, trustworthy, human" goal is carried by **copy, pacing, and human touches** layered onto that precise system — warmth in the writing and the moments of contact, not by softening the engineered aesthetic.

## Anti-references

- **Sterile enterprise / consultancy.** No stock-photo handshakes, no navy-and-gray corporate blandness, no Accenture-flavored abstraction. Nubis is technical and specific, not a slide deck.
- **AI-hype / chatbot startup.** No glowing orbs, neon cyberpunk, sci-fi clichés, or "revolutionary AI" breathlessness. The agent earns trust by being governable, not by looking magical.
- **Generic SaaS template.** No gradient-blob hero, no identical 3-card feature grid, no hero-metric template, no purple-on-white AI-slop landing page.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the oversight, don't claim it.** The differentiator is human governance of autonomous systems. Make it visible and interactive (the live agent, the oversight toggle, transparency signals) rather than stated in a bullet.
2. **Practice the resilience you sell.** The site itself should be fast, accessible, and unbreakable — the craft is part of the pitch. A flaky page undercuts "Architectural Resilience."
3. **Earn trust through specificity.** Name the mechanism, the tradeoff, the real Umbraco migration path. Concrete and candid beats polished and vague for this audience.
4. **Calm under precision.** The aesthetic is engineered and exact; the voice is the adult in the room. Hold both — never let precision tip into cold, never let warmth tip into hype.
5. **The agent is the argument.** The talking head is not decoration; it's the live demonstration that Nubis ships governed autonomy. Every interaction should reinforce capability-plus-control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA.** Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (watch muted ink tints on paper), large text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and alt text in the brand voice. Every animation needs a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (already scaffolded in globals.css). The 3D agent and voice features must have a usable non-JS / no-mic / reduced-motion fallback and not trap or block keyboard users.
